  • Fed — September now priced as a hold: Funds rate 3.50–3.75% under Chair Kevin Warsh, with three officials having dissented in favour of a hike in July. Markets now see a 65% probability of no change on Sept 15–16 after a run of weak US data. July 28–29 minutes release today, 2:00pm ET. Separately, the 30-year Treasury yield hit a 19-year high this week amid a global sovereign bond selloff — a stress signal, not a dollar-positive one. 🔴 Your largest structural drag, and it got heavier today.
