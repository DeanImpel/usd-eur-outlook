This is a marginal window that is fully hostage to tomorrow morning's CPI — do not convert the balance into a geopolitical bounce twelve hours before the print that decides the next month.

Iran's Foreign Minister confirmed Tehran is not in direct talks with Washington, and repeated that reopening requires the US to lift its naval blockade and pay compensation. Higher oil rebuilds the US inflation impulse and keeps the hawkish Fed case breathing — more euro per dollar — but it is a headline, not a trend.
  • The euro side keeps quietly improving. July HICP 2.9% YoY, deposit rate 2.40%, unemployment 6.3%, and Friday's second estimate is expected to confirm Q2 GDP growth of 0.4% — the strongest since early 2025. Markets price one more ECB hike by year-end. The euro is not just riding a weak dollar anymore; the Euro-area data has genuinely firmed, which works against you.

3. What's Driving It

  • Dollar — firm on oil, weak on fundamentals: DXY ~99.84, up ~0.2% on the day and +0.03% from Monday's close, holding a narrow band above 99.80. Resistance 100.00, support 99.30. Down 1.4% over the past month. 🟡 Mixed — the level helps you today, the one-month direction does not.
  • Fed — September hike odds ~50%, down from 67% a week ago: The July 29 decision was a 9–3 hold at 3.50–3.75% under Chair Kevin Warsh, with three dissenters wanting an immediate quarter point. Friday's −23K payrolls removed the labor-market cover for tightening and the market has not taken it back. Next decision Sept 15–16, with the dot plot. 🔴 Your largest active drag.
  • Strait of Hormuz — talks are not even happening: Iran's FM confirmed no direct US–Iran negotiations are underway; the Oman corridor draft remains unsigned, and Tehran's preconditions (blockade lifted, sanctions relief, war reparations) are unchanged. Brent hit $90 intraday, settling near $87.5 (+4.7%); WTI $83.62 (+1.8%); EU natural gas +11%. 🟢 Favorable today — a frozen standoff keeps oil elevated, which keeps the US inflation impulse and the hawkish Fed case alive. Also the single largest reversal risk: one signed corridor and this flips hard.
  • ECB — hiking bias intact, and now with growth behind it: Deposit rate 2.40%, unchanged in July after June's 25bp hike, the first in three years. July HICP came in at 2.9%, up from 2.8%. Q2 GDP second estimate (Fri) expected to confirm +0.4% QoQ. Next decision Sept 10. 🔴 A euro-supportive stance you are paying for.
  • Rate differential — level favors you, direction does not: Fed funds 3.75% vs ECB 2.40% — a 135bp gap still pulling capital toward the dollar. But the travel is Fed-dovish and ECB-hawkish, and 10-year Bunds at 3.18% vs Treasuries at 4.71% have been converging. 🔴 Structural, and it will not resolve before the September meetings.
