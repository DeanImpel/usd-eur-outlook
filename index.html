3. What's Driving It

  • Dollar — below 100 and staying there: DXY ~99.7, off 0.1% post-CPI, holding above Friday's two-month low of 99.5 but down 1.4% over the past month and now ten sessions below the 100 handle. Resistance 100.00, support 99.30. 🟡 Mixed — the level is tolerable, the trend is not.
  • Fed — September hike odds down to 40%: July CPI at 3.4% YoY with core at 2.5% removed the last inflation argument for tightening, on top of the −23K July payrolls print. Chair Kevin Warsh holds at 3.50–3.75% with three hawkish dissenters. Next decision Sept 15–16 with the dot plot. 🔴 Your largest active drag, and it deteriorated this week.
  • Strait of Hormuz — reopening chatter is back: Iran–Oman bilateral talks reportedly at an advanced stage; Pakistan's defense minister says the US and Iran are "close to some sort of arrangement." Trump counters that the US has "total control," and Wednesday's tanker attacks dented the optimism — but Brent still fell to $87.92 (−1.2%). Transits remain a fraction of the ~130/day pre-conflict run rate; Q2 flows averaged 4.9m b/d vs 21.6m b/d in 4Q25. 🔴 Now working against you — the direction of travel is toward reopening, which drops oil, drops the US inflation impulse, and drops your number.
  • ECB — hiking bias intact: Deposit rate 2.40%, unchanged in July after June's 25bp hike, the first in three years. July HICP 2.9%, up from 2.8%, with energy at 10.0% YoY. Next decision Sept 10. 🔴 A euro-supportive stance you are paying for.
  • Rate differential — level favors you, direction does not: Fed funds 3.75% vs ECB 2.40% — a 135bp gap still pulling capital toward the dollar. But Treasury yields fell again on CPI (2yr 4.176%, 10yr 4.652%) while Bunds held, and the travel is Fed-dovish, ECB-hawkish. 🔴 Structural, and it will not resolve before the September meetings.
