3. What's Driving It

  • Dollar — losing the 100 handle: DXY closed Friday at 99.67 (−0.30%) and is trading near 99.4 Monday, at the lower end of the monthly range and 2.2% below the late-July high of 101.60. Scotiabank flags the US 2/30s curve steepening to 108bps as "a further headwind for the USD," with near-term risk of a slip to the mid-98 area. 🔴 The level has broken and momentum is one-way.
  • Fed — September hike odds down to ~30%: July retail sales −0.6% (vs +0.1% consensus) on top of CPI 3.4% / core 2.5%, PPI 4.7% YoY and −23K payrolls. Chair Kevin Warsh holds at 3.50–3.75% with three hawkish dissenters. FOMC minutes land Wednesday Aug 19; next decision Sept 15–16 with the dot plot. 🔴 Your largest active drag and it deteriorated again over the weekend.
  • Strait of Hormuz — priced open, physically still shut: Roughly 14 vessels/day are transiting against a pre-war run rate near 120/day, despite the waterway being publicly declared open. Iran and Oman have a framework but the transit levy is unresolved (Iran 5–7% of cargo value vs Oman's ~3%) and Tehran ties normalization to US concessions. Brent has recovered to $88.52, WTI $82.40, EU gas above €60/MWh. 🟢 The one factor working for you — as long as the ships don't sail, the euro's energy-import drag persists.
  • ECB — hiking bias intact and now consensus: Deposit rate 2.40%, unchanged in July after June's 25bp hike, the first in three years. July HICP 2.9%, Q2 GDP +0.4%. Markets have moved from "possible" to "growing acceptance" of a final 25bp hike on Sept 10. Lagarde speaks Wednesday Aug 19. 🔴 A euro-supportive stance you are paying for on every transfer.
  • Rate differential — level favors you, direction does not: Fed funds 3.75% vs ECB 2.40% — a 135bp gap still pulling capital toward the dollar. But the US 10-year sits at 4.697% against Bunds at 3.199%, and every US print narrows the expected path. 🔴 Structural, and it will not resolve before the September meetings.
