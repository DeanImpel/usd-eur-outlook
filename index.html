Every dollar you convert today buys ~€0.880 — a small step up from yesterday, as the dollar continues to hold firm near a one-month high on Hormuz-driven safe-haven demand. 3. What's Driving It

  • Dollar holding near a one-month high: The Dollar Index sits at ~101.3, near its Jul 23 high of 101.5, up from the ~100.6–100.8 range earlier in the week. 🟢 Favorable for you — a firmer dollar buys more euros.
  • Strait of Hormuz — escalating, not stabilizing: 10 consecutive nights of US strikes on Iran, Iran resuming vessel attacks after exiting its prior understanding with the US, and a fresh Houthi threat against Saudi ports. Non-Iranian ship transits remain down ~90% YoY. 🟢 The dominant driver right now — escalation typically strengthens the dollar via safe-haven flows, though a sudden ceasefire could reverse this quickly.
  • European gas prices — still elevated: TTF natural gas remains near €63.92/MWh, its highest level since January 2023, up more than 45% since the start of July on reduced Persian Gulf LNG flows. 🔴 for the euro — a growing cost drag on the eurozone economy, though a background factor rather than today's direct price mover.
  • ECB — off the board until September: The ECB last held its main rate steady; some market commentary now flags rising odds of a September move as a live possibility, though this isn't yet a strong consensus call. Next scheduled ECB meeting: Sept 10. 🟡 Not a near-term catalyst, but worth tracking — a hawkish ECB surprise in September would work against you.
  • Fed — the dominant near-term catalyst, days away: Fed meets Jul 28–29, decision due Jul 29. A hawkish hold/statement would be favorable for you; any dovish pivot on rate-cut timing would work against further gains. 🟡 The key event risk over the next week.
