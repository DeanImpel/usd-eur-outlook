3. What's Driving It

  • Dollar — flat today, firmer this week: The Dollar Index sits at ~101.53, essentially unchanged today (-0.01%) but up from 101.18 yesterday, as markets stay cautious ahead of the Fed's decision tomorrow. 🟡 Mixed — not a strong signal in either direction right now, but the tape is leaning slightly dollar-supportive into the meeting.
  • Strait of Hormuz — still critical, not stabilizing: Non-Iranian ship transits remain near 25 vessels vs. a 108 pre-crisis baseline (~90% YoY decline), and Houthi forces continue threatening a Saudi-port "maritime embargo" that could extend into the Red Sea. 🟢 The dominant driver right now — ongoing tension typically supports the dollar via safe-haven flows, though a sudden ceasefire could reverse this quickly.
  • European gas prices — elevated, off the recent peak: TTF natural gas trades near €58/MWh today, up 1.52% d/d but down from last week's €63/MWh spike (highest since January 2023), on reduced Persian Gulf LNG flows tied to the Hormuz crisis. 🔴 for the euro — a real, if slightly easing, cost drag on the eurozone economy.
  • ECB — off the board until September: The ECB's last Governing Council meeting was July 23 (hawkish hold at a 2.25% deposit rate, keeping a September hike live); the ECB does not meet in August, so the next monetary policy decision is expected September 10, 2026. 🟡 Not a near-term catalyst, but worth tracking.
  • Fed — the dominant near-term catalyst, tomorrow: Fed meets Jul 28–29, decision due tomorrow, Jul 29 at 2:00pm ET (press conference 2:30pm ET). A hawkish hold/statement would be favorable for you; any dovish pivot on rate-cut timing would work against further gains. 🟡 The key event risk over the next 24 hours.
